## 🚀 Overview
Powerful editing doesn't have to be complicated, and the PowerDirector app for Windows is proof. CyberLink designed it so newcomers can get great results quickly while the full depth of a professional editor stays available underneath. Start from a template or use guided, one-click tools to trim clips, add effects, and drop in music — then, when you're ready, explore the complete multi-track timeline, color controls, and audio tools at your own pace. Because it's the same PowerDirector video editor that pros rely on, the app scales with your skills instead of holding you back. It's an easy, capable way to turn everyday footage into finished videos on a Windows PC.

## ✨ Features
- **Guided editing tools** — one-click features and clear menus help you edit quickly, even with no prior experience.
- **Templates and presets** — start from ready-made designs to get a polished look, then customize as much as you like.
- **Full timeline underneath** — step up to multi-track editing, color tools, and audio controls whenever you want more power.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Launch the PowerDirector app on your Windows PC and create a new project.
2. Import your clips, photos, and audio into the media area.
3. Use a template or drag your footage onto the timeline to build your video.
4. Add effects, transitions, music, and titles with the guided tools.
5. Preview the result and export it, ready to share.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is PowerDirector free?**
Yes, PowerDirector has a free version so you can start editing at no cost. Paid editions add more premium effects and content, but the free download covers a full editing workflow.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
The PowerDirector app runs on 64-bit Windows, mainly Windows 10 and Windows 11. A currently supported version is recommended.

**Do I need editing experience to use PowerDirector?**
No. The app offers guided tools and templates that make it easy to start, while the full timeline and advanced features are there as you grow.

**Is my data secure?**
PowerDirector lets you edit locally on your Windows PC. Sign in with a trusted account, keep the app updated, and manage your exported videos like any other files on your computer.
